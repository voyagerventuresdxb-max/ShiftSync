# Staff quick start

For staff. You need your phone and the join link from your manager. No app download.

## 1. Join your venue

1. Tap the join link your manager sent you. The page says **Join <venue> as staff**. (screenshot: 70-join-link-390.png)
2. Enter your mobile number → **Send code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code. The first time, also enter your full name. Tap **Verify & continue**. (screenshot: 71-join-phone-and-code-390.png)
4. If your manager already added you, you are in. If not, you see **Waiting for <manager> to approve you**. (screenshot: 72-join-pending-390.png)
5. When you are approved, open ShiftSync and tap **Staff sign in**. Sign in with your number and a new code. You see **You're in**, then My Shifts. (screenshot: 73-join-youre-in-390.png)

**No code arrived?** Text sign-in codes may not be switched on yet. Ask your manager to send you a **login link**. Tap it, then tap **Sign in**. It works once.

## 2. Your screens

- **My Shifts** (the calendar button at the top): your next shifts, in venue time. (screenshot: 61-staff-my-shifts-390.png)
- **Home**: announcements and shoutouts from your team. (screenshot: 60-staff-home-390.png)
- **Scheduling → Personal Rota**: your whole week, day by day. A shift marked **Draft — not yet published** can still change.
- The **bell** at the top shows your notifications.

## 3. Mark your availability

My Shifts → **Your availability this week**. Tap a day to change it:
unmarked → **Unavailable** → **Prefer off** → unmarked. Your manager sees these marks when building the rota.

## 4. Ask for cover

1. Scheduling → **Personal Rota** → find the shift → **Request cover**.
2. Pick the colleague who will cover → **Send request**.
3. Your manager approves or declines it. Requests for a week close **Wednesday 17:00**, venue time.

## 5. Add ShiftSync to your home screen

- **iPhone (Safari):** open ShiftSync → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**. Open it from the new icon and sign in again there.
- **Android (Chrome):** open ShiftSync → menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## 6. Notifications on your phone (if switched on)

People → **Notification preferences** → **Enable notifications** → **Allow**.
On iPhone this works only from the Home Screen icon (iOS 16.4 or later). If it says push is not switched on yet, your notifications still appear under the bell.

## Signed out?

You see "You've been signed out." Sign in again with your number, or ask your manager for a new login link.
