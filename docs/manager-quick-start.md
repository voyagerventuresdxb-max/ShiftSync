# Manager quick start

For venue owners and managers. Use your phone's browser. Only features that work today are here.

## 1. Create your venue

Open ShiftSync. After the Welcome screen there are five steps: **Account → Venue → Roster → Review → Invite**.
(screenshots: 01-onboarding-welcome-390.png to 05-onboarding-invite-390.png)

1. **Account:** your mobile number → **Send code** → the code, your name and the venue name → **Verify & continue**.
2. **Venue:** name, location and venue type. Floor sections come later, on the **Floor plan** page: upload your plan, tap where each section is and name it.
3. **Roster:** upload your roster file, or **Skip for now, I'll add staff later**.
4. **Review:** check every flagged row. **Confirm & Continue** works when nothing is flagged. Nothing is live until you confirm.
5. **Invite:** share the venue join link on WhatsApp, or save the QR code. **Finish setup**.

Rename the venue any time: tap the round badge at the top left (Profile) → **Venue** → **Rename venue** → **Save**. Your header changes at once; your team sees the new name the next time they open or switch back to ShiftSync.

No code arrived? Ask ShiftSync to set up the venue for you. You get a one-time login link that opens setup at the Venue screen.

## 2. Share the join link

People → **Join link** → **Copy link**, **WhatsApp** or **Save QR**. **Regenerate** sets how long it works and how many people can use it. **Revoke** stops it at once.

## 3. Approve people

People → **Pending Approvals** → **Approve** or **Decline**. (screenshot: 40-people-pending-approval-390.png)
Someone you already added in the Staff Directory, with their phone number, gets in without approval.

## 4. Upload a roster

Scheduling → **Upload Roster**. Excel, CSV and text PDFs are read on ShiftSync's own server.
- Photos and scans need the AI reader. It asks before it sends anything. It may not be switched on yet: then upload a spreadsheet, or add staff by hand (People → **Add staff member**).
- Fix rows under **Needs Review**, then **Mark reviewed**. To change a name or role, fix the file and upload it again.
- **Confirm & Commit** saves the shifts.

## 5. Build and publish the week

Scheduling → **Weekly rota builder**. Tap **+** on a day to add a shift, or tap a shift to change it.
- **Publish & notify** sends the week. Each person with a shift that week gets a notification.
- After a change the badge says **Unpublished changes**. Tap **Publish changes**.
- **Save as template** and **Templates** reuse a week.

## 6. Cover requests

Staff ask for cover from their rota. You get a notification. Home → **Conflict-Free Approvals** → **Approve** or **Decline**. Approve moves the shift. Requests for a week close Wednesday 17:00, venue time.

## 7. Announcements and shoutouts

Home → **Announcements** → **Post** → write → **Broadcast**. Everyone at the venue is notified.
Home → **Shoutouts** → **Tag a shift** → a colleague, their shift, a note → **Give shoutout**. (screenshot: 10-manager-home-390.png)

## 8. Voice commands (if switched on)

Tap the round button above the bottom bar, speak, then tap again. A sheet shows what will happen. Nothing changes until you tap **Confirm**. Try "Publish this week's rota". If voice is off, a message says so: use the buttons.

## Help your team sign in

- People → Staff Directory → **Send login link**. It works once and expires (24 hours by default).
- Set someone **Inactive** to sign them out everywhere at once. Managers can change staff. Venue owners can change managers and staff.
